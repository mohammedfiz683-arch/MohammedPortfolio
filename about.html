INTERACTION DESIGNER

Building systems through games and experiences.

Hi, I'm Mohammed. I sense the mood of a room before anyone speaks, and design taught me to turn that instinct into experiences.

After studying science and computer science, I chose Interaction Design at Anant National University because I cared more about people than technology. Since then, I've made narrative games, VR installations, zines, board games and UX research.

In my version of the world, everything is gamified. Whether it's built from bricks or run on tricks, every piece has rules, rewards and players, and even chaos is a puzzle people can solve together. That's why I believe design, through storytelling and play, can do good in the world.

If you're a studio, a research lab or a fellow maker, let's talk.
