Shatter

A narrative RPG exploring society and disillusionment through interactive storytelling.

GAME DESIGN NARRATIVE DESIGN INTERACTION DESIGN
PLAY THE PROTOTYPE FULLSCREEN

A 2D narrative-driven RPG created during a 48-hour game jam. Shatter explores themes of society, identity, and disillusionment through player choices and interactive storytelling.

? PROJECT DETAILS

Type: 2D Narrative RPG

Role: Dev, Design, Narrative

Duration: 48 Hours

Platform: Desktop

Tools: GDevelop

Mode: Single Player

THE CORE IDEA

The game is built around the idea of 'shattering illusions'. As players progress, they uncover deeper layers of reality, where choices reveal consequences and challenge perception.

GAME DESIGN APPROACH

Narrative as Gameplay

Story unfolds through player decisions.

Choices Matter

Each interaction shapes experience.

Exploration-Driven

Players discover meaning through movement.

Emotional Engagement

Focus on reflection, not just progression.

KEY SYSTEMS

  • Dialogue-based interaction system
  • Choice-driven progression
  • Scene-based storytelling
  • Visual character feedback

BUILDING THE EXPERIENCE

  • Designed branching story paths
  • Created character-driven narrative moments
  • Used simple mechanics to support storytelling
  • Focused on pacing and emotional flow

ITERATION / TESTING

Playtesting helped refine narrative clarity and player understanding. Adjustments were made to improve flow and reduce confusion in key story moments.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION

This game was developed during a 48-hour game jam, so it is not fully polished but focuses on core concept and experience.
