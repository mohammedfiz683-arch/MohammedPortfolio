Nothing Phone 3a
Software Redesign

A premium case study overhauling the Nothing OS widget ecosystem and notification panel mechanics. Introducing tactile feedback, micro-interactions, on-device intelligence, and clean retro-inspired information hierarchies.

SLIDE 01 / PAC-MAN WIDGET

Pac Man Widget

A 2x2 interactive widget that brings the iconic Pac-Man game right to your home screen. Just like the Snake widget, it's a fun, tap-and-play experience that lives inside your layout—perfect for quick breaks, a bit of nostalgia, or just adding a bit of joy to your day, the Nothing way.

Nothing Brand Philosophy

Integrating classic retro game logic into clean, grid-aligned desktop modules, transforming idle screens into playful micro-experiences.

Design States (From Presentation)
SLIDE 02 / PANEL OVERHAUL

Brightness Slider Redesign

A sleek, responsive brightness control with smooth transitions and real-time feedback, crafted in Nothing's minimalist style. Previously hidden behind a second swipe, the slider now appears right on the first pull of the notification shade—exactly where users expect it. We've also overhauled the UI for a cleaner, more intuitive experience.

Key Panel Updates

• Relocated to first shade swipe, eliminating the double swipe friction.
• Thick, visual slider bar with high-contrast, light/dark responsive indicators.

Notification panel overhaul (From Presentation)
SLIDE 03 / BRIGHTNESS WIDGETS

Brightness Slider Widget

A compact, always-on widget that displays current brightness levels with dynamic visual cues. It adapts to time of day and ambient conditions, providing at-a-glance awareness of your screen's light output.

Visual Cues & Adaptability

Integrates a bespoke dot-matrix sun icon that expands or contracts representing system light values, scaling elegantly across both 4x4 square and 2x2 capsules.

SLIDE 04 / EMOTIVE STATUS

Battery Mood Tracker

The previous widget felt very empty and lacked personality. Our design: more than a battery meter—this widget gives your device a bit of emotion. It tracks power levels and usage patterns, deploying mood-like indicators (happy, tired, charging, etc.) to make battery status more relatable and engaging.

Original vs Our Design

Replaces a static battery outline with reactive, highly minimal expressions directly responsive to system charging logs and percentage states.

Key Case Study Reflections

Overhauling a high-concept UI style such as Nothing OS requires balancing tactile personality and minimal constraints. Replacing empty, sterile slots with responsive widgets (like Pac-Man game flows, custom adaptive brightness nodes, and battery tracker battery state expressions) adds real delight to the operating system's interface while fully adhering to their core branding.

1. Minimalist Constraints

Curated accent layouts and crisp, dot-matrix typefaces guarantee that mobile dashboards load clean, responsive metrics under any light conditions.

2. Direct Presentation Reference

Re-creating the actual presentation frames (Blinky ghosts, sun indices, user flow query charts) delivers a highly authentic design case study.
